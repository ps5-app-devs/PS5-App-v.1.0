Support

Quick answers about how Game Launcher works with the apps you already use.

Does it replace Steam or the Epic Games Launcher?

No. It works on top of them. Your games still start through Steam and Epic, so saves, cloud sync, friends and online play keep working as before.

Which games does it find?

Every game in every Steam library on your PC, Steam games you own but haven't installed, your Epic Games library, and any game or folder of games you add yourself.

Do I need a controller?

No. A controller is the best way to use it, but the keyboard and mouse work everywhere.

How do I open the Control Center over a game?

Press the Home button on your controller while you play. It opens on top of the game and closes the same way.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is it safe?

SmartScreen shows this for new apps that aren't widely downloaded yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. If your browser blocks the download, choose Keep.

Where is my data kept?

Only on your PC. The app downloads pictures and store details, and never uploads your library.

Make your PC feel like a console.

Free for Windows 10 and 11. Works with Steam, Epic Games and any game on your PC.
